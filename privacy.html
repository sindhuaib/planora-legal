Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

When a customer books an appointment through Planora, we collect their name, email address, and phone number, along with the selected date and time, to create and manage the booking.

Google Calendar access

Merchants who connect their Google account grant Planora access to create events on their Google Calendar. This access is used solely to create a calendar event with a Google Meet link for each confirmed booking, and to invite the customer to that event by email. We do not read, modify, or delete any other events on the merchant's calendar, and we do not share calendar data with any third party.

Data storage

Booking and scheduling data is stored securely and is only accessible to the merchant who owns the store the booking was made on.
